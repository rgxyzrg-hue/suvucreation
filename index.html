Our story

A small stall, a lot of love

Suvu Creation began with a table on a busy Hardoi street and a basket of handmade flowers. Day by day, the stall grew: crochet sunflowers, Korean-style bags, satin scrunchies, evil-eye charms, and gifts that people kept coming back for.

Everything is still made the same way, by hand, with care, in small batches.
